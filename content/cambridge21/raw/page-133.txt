      This response addressed the requirements of the task but, overall, there is a lack
      of detail.
      The first paragraph is an introduction to the diagram. Task 1 Academic tasks
      require an overview, a summary of the key stages of the process, but this is
      missing. Paragraph 2 contains some irrelevant information [This is important
      because the process have a lot of steps and the distance gives time to all of
      them]. The main response comes in paragraphs 3 and 4 and it does include the
      main features, but there are details missing, such as 'clouds form' 'Windward side'
      and 'Leeward side'. The irrelevant content and missing details detract from the
      score for this task.
      Information is generally arranged coherently and there are linking expressions
      [First of all I Moreover I Then] and some referencing within the process [this
      desert I all of them I this part]. There is some repetition [The first I First of all] but
      overall, cohesive devices are used to some good effect.
      Vocabulary is generally good and appropriate for the task [appears I explanation],
      the meaning is clear but there are errors [arrove / arrive I grow up I move up I
      saids I sees] and a lack of precision [in specific I specifically I gives time I be/ow
      part]. Similarly, the grammatical range is narrow, often inaccurate and repetitive
      but the writer does attempt multi clause sentences [while I when] and a modal
      verb [can] so there is some ability. Overall though, grammatical errors are frequent
      and they do cause some difficulty in understanding.
      To achieve a higher score, an overview, which summarises the main stages of the
      process should be presented.
